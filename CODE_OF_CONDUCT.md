# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors and maintainers commit to providing a respectful, inclusive, and
professional environment.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully;
- welcome constructive technical disagreement;
- distinguish evidence from inference;
- respect privacy, authorship, and licensing;
- avoid harassment and personal attacks; and
- focus feedback on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
deliberate exposure of private information, plagiarism, misrepresentation of
scientific evidence, or knowingly distributing restricted material.

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository maintainers.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may limit
participation when necessary to protect the community or repository.
